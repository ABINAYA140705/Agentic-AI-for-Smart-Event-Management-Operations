About EventOps AI

An autonomous event management intelligence platform that uses agentic AI to plan, monitor and optimise events from registration to feedback.

Our Mission

Event organisers spend hours on repetitive coordination: managing venues, speakers, sponsors, attendees and last-minute incidents. EventOps AI automates this work with cooperating AI agents so teams can focus on delivering great experiences.

Our Vision

A future where events run themselves: issues are detected early, resources are allocated intelligently, and decisions are backed by real-time data and AI insights.
